How I decide
  1. Build when it's within reach and adds real value. Buy when a mature tool is worth the cost.
  2. No outside developers. I build or configure it myself.
  3. Small, connectable pieces (Zapier or n8n) over one big system.
  4. Client and financial data privacy, and backups, come first.
